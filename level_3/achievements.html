CTE Pre-Engineering Leadership and Achievement Award

The Career and Technical Education Pre-Engineering Leadership Award is presented to exceptional students who demonstrate remarkable leadership and technical prowess in the Edmonds School District's CTE program. The students are selected by their CTE teachers and are approved by members of their Advisory Committee. Issued by Edmonds School District CTE Program - Jun 2023
