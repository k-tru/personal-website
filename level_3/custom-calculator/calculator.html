Compound Interest Calculator

What is it?

When you hold your in a savings account, it accumulates something known as interest. This is money the bank pays you for letting them hold your money for you. Compount interest is the interest you earn on the sum of your principal and interest. This calculator is designed to calculate the time in years it will take to reach your goal!

Some important vocabulary:

  • Principal: the starting amount or balance
  • Interest Rate: the rate at which your money will grow
  • End Balance: sum of money you end with after interest
  • Additional Contribution: money added yearly to deposit

Note: Additional Contribution only works for final amount calculations currently.
